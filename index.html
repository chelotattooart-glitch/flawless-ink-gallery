Planning your visit

Where is the tattoo studio?

Find Flawless Ink Gallery at 7913 W Sample Rd, Coral Springs, FL 33065. See directions and studio hours.

How do I request a consultation?

Complete the consultation form with your tattoo idea, placement, approximate size and preferred artist. The form prepares an email: review it and tap Send in your email app. The studio must confirm your appointment.

Can I visit without an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, subject to artist availability. Call (754) 306-8422 to check availability before visiting.

What should I include with my tattoo idea?

Include the subject, style, placement, approximate size and whether you prefer color or black and grey. You can add a reference link in the form and attach photos in your email app before sending.

About Marcelo

Born in Argentina, I moved to the United States in 2016 with a dream: to elevate my craft, connect with talented artists, and push my creativity beyond new horizons.

Over the past decade, I’ve dedicated myself to growing as an artist and turning that dream into a reality. One of my proudest milestones has been opening my own studio, Flawless ink., a space created with the vision of bringing artists together in a peaceful, supportive environment where they can develop their skills, express their creativity, and grow beyond their own limits.

I specialize in black and grey realism, with a deep passion for creating artwork that carries meaning and emotion. Whether I’m bringing a portrait to life or creating a memorial piece to honor someone who is no longer with us, my goal is to transform skin into a canvas that tells a story.

For me, tattooing is more than creating an image—it’s about creating a connection, preserving memories, and bringing happiness to every person who trusts me with their story.

My name is Marcelo Ezequiel Salva, and this is more than my profession. This is my art, my journey, and my passion.

About Adrian

Hello, I’m Adrian Ramirez and I was born in San Jose, Costa Rica. I grew up in South Florida for the second half of my life. My journey as an artist began in my early years and evolved into fulfilling career in tattooing.

After dedicating two years as an apprentice, I’ve spent the past seven years as a professional artist honing my craft and understanding the science behind a tattoo. I pride myself in making high quality work no matter how big or how small the piece is. My favorite style to tattoo is Black and Gray realism. My expertise spans various other styles, including llustrative, Black Outs, Color, and Geometric Tattoos.

Being surrounded by tattoos day in and day out, some artists in the industry tend to be desensitized to the clients experience, however this is not the case with me. I approach each client with fresh perspective and genuine care. I treat every tattoo as though it’s on my own skin, ensuring a unique and personal experience. Whether this is your first tattoo or you’re adding to an existing collection, I strive to make each piece a work of art.

About Sammi

My name is Samantha, but most people call me Sammy. I'm 33 years old, and I'm a licensed tattoo artist based right here in South Florida.

I've been an artist my entire life painting, drawing, building things and never being afraid to get my hands dirty. Growing up, I was constantly surrounded by tattoos via my dad going to tattoo conventions and shops quite often and art from my grandmother who drew and painted also. Being exposed to that creative world as a child heavily influenced me and naturally inspired my path to becoming the artist I am today.

I've been officially tattooing for 3 years now. I earned and finished my apprenticeship at Flawless Ink in Coral Springs, and I'm proud to say I've been holding it down there ever since!

If you sit in my chair, you'll quickly find out that I'm outgoing, funny, and incredibly easy to talk to. I genuinely love meeting every single one of you. Being trusted to create beautiful memories and permanent art for you means the world to me.

My Specialities:

  • Fine line work
  • Illustrative realism
  • Blackwork ornamentals

I absolutely love bringing your custom ideas to life, but I also have a ton of pre made flash that I'm dying to tattoo!

Ready for some new ink? Drop me a DM to chat about your ideas, or head straight to the booking form linked in my bio. Let's make something amazing I'd love to be your new artist!

About Kimi

Hi everyone!! I’m Kimi, a Vietnamese-Am artist born and raised in Broward. I’ve been tattooing for almost 3 years now 💫 I’m super excited to be a part of the team!

🌱 Some things about me:

I love working with my clients to mesh my creative direction & style with their ideas! Collaborating is the best part of the process for me — it’s always super satisfying to see our ideas come to life together! :)

My style is mainly illustrative fine-line. My favorite thing to tattoo is anything I find cute!! I love tattooing things with texture/effects, cute characters, nature, whimsical things 💫 I love tattoos that flow well with the body and I’m open to any of your ideas. I also enjoy illustrating outside of tattooing too!

I hope that my care translates to my tattooing process and I love to create a space for everyone to feel comfortable in having a say in the process 🤍 I believe having a good tattoo is important but feeling heard/cared for & having a good experience is just as important!

Thank you for checking out my work and reading all of this!! Super excited to (hopefully 😎) work with you in the future :)
